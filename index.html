03 · Why both

A loop, not two tools.

The two products are the same idea at two speeds. Numbers tell you whether a system works; replay tells you whether you could have followed it. Most systems die on the second question — and no one else sells both ends of it.

Test

Prove it on eleven years

The Machine runs the rules across every regime and hands back the numbers.

Watch

See it fire, bar by bar

Replay the trades the report lists and find out what they felt like at the time.

Change

Fix what you saw

Adjust the rules and run it again. Either half alone is a tool; the loop is the product.
